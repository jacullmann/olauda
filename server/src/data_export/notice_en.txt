==========================================

This archive is our answer to your right of access (Art. 15 GDPR) and your
right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR). It contains two files:

  README.txt  this explanation
  data.json   all data stored for your account, in a structured,
              machine-readable format (JSON, UTF-8). You can open it in any
              text editor or import it into another service.

All times in data.json are ISO 8601 timestamps in Coordinated Universal Time
(UTC). The export is free of charge, and you can download it again at any
time in your account settings.


1. What data.json contains
--------------------------

account            Your account: email address, whether a password and
                   two-factor authentication are set up, settings (language,
                   theme, personalization), sign-up and last sign-in, and the
                   randomly generated name other members see you as.
linkedAccounts     Sign-in services linked to your account (e.g. Google) with
                   your account ID and email address at that service.
groups             Your group memberships and roles, when you last opened each
                   group, your course selection, bans in groups and invite
                   links you created, used or revoked.
content            What you created yourself: tasks and exams, uploaded files
                   and group pictures, announcements, chat messages and your
                   private tasks (decrypted).
interactions       Tasks you checked off, pinned, archived or kept, and
                   announcements you have read.
reports            Reports you filed and reports about content you created.
security           Your passkeys, when your recovery codes were created and
                   used, your sessions (signed-in devices) with IP addresses
                   and browser identifiers, security events with IP address
                   and browser identifier (e.g. sign-ins and failed sign-in
                   attempts, changes to your password, passkeys and two-factor
                   authentication, creating a group, accepting an invite),
                   requested password reset codes and confirmation codes.
activityLog        Your account's activity log.

Uploaded files are listed under content.files with a link to the original
file. Download them soon if you need them: files are deleted automatically
once the task they are attached to is deleted.


2. Purposes and legal bases
---------------------------

- Account, settings, linked sign-in services, groups, course selection and all
  content: providing the service under our terms of use
  (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Sessions, security events, failed sign-in attempts and emailed codes:
  protecting your account and the service against abuse and unauthorized
  access (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
- Activity log: investigating errors and abuse and measuring how many people
  use the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Reports: reviewing reported content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).


3. Recipients
-------------

- Members of your groups see the content you publish there, your role and
  your randomly generated name, but not your email address.
- Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) runs our servers and database on our behalf.
- Cloudinary Inc. (USA) stores uploaded images and files on our behalf.
- Resend, Inc. (USA) sends emails about your account on our behalf.
- Google receives the data required for signing in with Google and processes
  it as an independent controller.
- Microsoft receives your IP address, browser identifier and the file's
  address when you open an Office file preview, and processes them as an
  independent controller.

We do not sell data or share it for advertising.


4. Transfers to third countries
-------------------------------

Cloudinary and Resend are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the European
Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under
which both companies are certified (Art. 45 GDPR). The same applies to Google
and Microsoft where they transfer data to the USA.


5. Retention
------------

- Account data, group memberships, course selection and private tasks: until
  you delete your account.
- Sessions: about 7 days after they were last used.
- Activity log: 30 days.
- Security events: 90 days, even if you delete your account before then.
- Chat messages: 7 days.
- Tasks and exams in groups: 90 days after their due date.
- Password reset codes: 30 minutes; unconfirmed sign-ups with their
  confirmation codes: 2 days.
- Reports: until we have reviewed them.
- All other group content: until you, the group admins or we delete it, or the
  group is deleted.

If you delete your account, tasks and announcements you created in groups stay
available to the group but are no longer linked to your account.


6. Source of the data
---------------------

The data comes from you and from your use of the service. If you sign in with
Google, we received your Google account ID and email address from Google.
Roles, bans in groups and notes on your tasks may come from the admins or
moderators of your groups.


7. Automated decision-making
----------------------------

No automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR), takes
place.


8. What this export deliberately leaves out
-------------------------------------------

- Access secrets: your password hash, your two-factor secret, your recovery
  codes, session tokens, invite tokens and password reset codes. They would put your account at risk
  if the file fell into the wrong hands. Whether they exist is stated in
  data.json (e.g. hasPassword, mfaEnabled).
- Information about other people: who reported your content and why, who used
  your invites, banned you or changed your role, including that person's IP
  address and browser identifier, and who created other content. This protects
  their rights (Art. 15(4) GDPR, Section 29(1) sentence 2 BDSG).
- Data not linked to your account: technical access data recorded when the
  website and app are loaded, and the aggregated analytics statistics (Umami).
- Data in your browser: language, theme and similar settings in your device's
  local storage. You can view and clear these in your browser.


9. Your rights
--------------

You have the right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR) and to object to processing based on
our legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You can delete your account yourself
at any time in your account settings. For anything else, email us at
kontakt@schul-dashboard.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection
supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the
Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information
(Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit),
Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany.

Our privacy policy has the full details:
https://schul-dashboard.com/legal/privacy-policy
